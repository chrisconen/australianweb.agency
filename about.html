01

You meet Chris. Real conversation, not a script. He pulls the goals, the constraints, the "this thing that scares me" — everything no AI can sense yet. He briefs Claude and Gemini in our shared context system.

◇ The Covenant ◇

What we promise you.

Working with AI shouldn't feel like a gamble. Here's the doctrine we hold ourselves to — written down, on the record, enforceable.

Your Data, Your Rules

We store visitor conversations on infrastructure you own (Cloudflare D1, AU region). No data ever trains a public AI model. Full export anytime — SQL dump in one command.

Always on the Record

Every AI decision is logged with reasoning. You can audit any output, ask "why did Grok write it like this?", and get a real answer — not "the algorithm decided."

100% Ownership

Every line of code, every design asset, every piece of content is yours upon final payment. No license fees, no SaaS lock-in, no "what happens if you stop paying us." It's all yours.

Auditable Stack

No black-box AI. Everything we build runs on open infrastructure (Cloudflare, n8n, your own Anthropic + Google API keys). Cancel us tomorrow — your system keeps running.

Human in the Loop

AI proposes, human approves. Chris sees every flagged lead, signs off on copy that goes live, and is the final word on anything that touches your reputation. AI doesn't ship alone.

Speed With Conscience

We're fast because AI compounds — not because we cut corners. Every build has tests, every launch has a rollback plan, every system has a "kill switch" you control directly.
