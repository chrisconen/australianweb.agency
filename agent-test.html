How it worksWhat the test checks

  • Find: whether the crawlers behind ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, Copilot, Claude and Perplexity may read your pages, and whether your firewall turns them away.
  • Understand: whether your content is readable without JavaScript and described in structured data: who you are, what you sell, prices and stock.
  • Act: whether agents can complete actions: a Universal Commerce Protocol profile for checkout in Google AI Mode and Gemini, WebMCP tools, and labelled forms.
  • This is an outside-in check. It cannot see your Google Merchant Center feed or Business Profile, or how often assistants actually mention you.
