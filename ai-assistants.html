It answers from your pages and documents, and it can show the source. Where that material is silent, or you want a person to decide, it hands over. The plumbing demo on this page does that for an exact quote. We set those rules with you.

Does the Privacy Act cover a small business?

The OAIC says the Privacy Act 1988 covers organisations with annual turnover of more than $3 million and Australian Government agencies. Most small businesses with annual turnover of $3 million or less are not covered, except listed cases: health service providers, trading in personal information, Commonwealth contracts and residential tenancy databases. We work to that guidance. Ask a lawyer.

What privacy-policy change starts on 10 December 2026?

The OAIC said on 30 September 2026 that from 10 December 2026, APP 1.8 will require an APP entity's privacy policy to state the kinds of personal information a program uses to make, or substantially help make, a decision that could significantly affect a person's rights or interests, and the decisions involved. We work to APP 1.8; ask a lawyer.

If a chatbot misleads a customer, are we liable?

The ACCC's submission to the Parliamentary Joint Select Committee on Artificial Intelligence says a business would be liable under the Australian Consumer Law for a misleading representation by an AI customer-service chatbot, just as for its website or an employee. We keep answers tied to your sources and hand over rather than guess. Ask a lawyer about your own obligations.
