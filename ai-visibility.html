Questions

Good to know.

Do Australian shoppers actually use AI to choose what to buy?

Deloitte’s 2026 Retail Report, cited on 1 September 2026, said 45% of consumers use AI to compare products and 38% use it to research options. eCommerceNews Australia reported on 2 October 2026 that half of 809 Australians surveyed had used AI to research a purchase.

Can customers check out inside Google’s AI results in Australia?

Inside Retail reported on 17 June 2026 that Australia was the first Asia-Pacific country to get Google’s Universal Commerce Protocol, with selected retailers. Google Merchant Center Help, retrieved 17 October 2026, says UCP checkout applies to eligible products in Australia for select merchants. We prepare the technical work and you apply.

Will this put my pages into AI training data?

No. We follow each publisher’s own split. Google’s crawler docs, updated 15 October 2026, say Google-Extended is a robots.txt token for Gemini training and grounding, and that it does not affect Google Search. OpenAI’s bots docs separate OAI-SearchBot from the GPTBot training crawler. We set the tokens you choose.

Does Google say we must do special optimisation for AI Overviews?

Google’s Search Central guide, updated 10 December 2025, says AI Overviews and AI Mode link to indexed pages that can show a snippet. It says there are no extra technical requirements. We still fix crawl blocks, markup and feeds so those pages can be found and quoted accurately.

What is WebMCP, and is it finished?

Chrome’s WebMCP docs, updated 7 October 2026, describe a proposed standard for exposing tools to browser agents. It is in a Chrome origin trial and Chrome says the API can change. The 16 October 2026 spec is a W3C Community Group draft, not a W3C Standard. We build to the current draft and retest when it changes.
