Questions

Good to know.

Which tools can you connect?

Common Australian stacks include Shopify or WooCommerce, Xero or MYOB, Australia Post or Sendle, Stripe or Square, HubSpot or Zoho, and booking tools such as Cliniko or Timely. If your tool has an API or webhook, we can usually reach it.

Do you send marketing email and SMS?

We build the sending flow. The ACMA says you need consent before sending marketing email or SMS, including when another party sends them on your behalf, and every message needs a working unsubscribe. We work to that guidance. This is not legal advice.

Does the Privacy Act apply to a small business?

The OAIC says the Privacy Act 1988 covers organisations with annual turnover over $3 million, and government agencies. Many smaller businesses are outside it, with listed exceptions such as health providers. We build to the guidance and suggest you take legal advice.

Have you built this before?

Yes. Chris Conen has built invoicing and shipping-label automation for Marina Yacht Sport’s new online store (launching late 2026), plus a lead endpoint that routes enquiries and scheduled monthly rescans for a compliance SaaS, both already running in production.
