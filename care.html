Questions

Good to know.

What does care include after launch?

Uptime, speed and security monitoring with alerts, updates and backups, SSL and security headers, technical SEO, GA4 and Search Console, AI visibility checks, and monthly improvements with a plain-English report. Hosting is on Cloudflare or Vercel.

How do you judge whether a page is fast?

Google's web.dev article, last updated 7 May 2025, rates the 75th percentile of page loads. Good is LCP at or under 2,500 ms, INP at or under 200 ms and CLS at or under 0.1. Poor is LCP over 4,000 ms, INP over 500 ms and CLS over 0.25. Anything between those cut-offs is rated 'needs improvement'.

Do you follow the Essential Eight?

Patching and regular backups are two of the eight strategies in the Australian Signals Directorate’s Essential Eight, and we apply both. ASD’s newer Essentials series is written mainly for government and large organisations; for smaller sites we work from ASD’s small business cyber security guidance. This is not legal advice.

What about analytics and tracking pixels?

The OAIC's 4 November 2024 guidance says the Privacy Act does not prohibit tracking pixels and groups pixels with cookies. Organisations must comply with the APPs. In its 2023 survey, 69% of adults did not think personal information used for tracking, profiling and targeted advertising was fair and reasonable. We work to that guidance. Seek legal advice on your notices.

Who does the work?

You deal with Chris Conen. He leads a team of AI agents: Architect and Astra write, test and review code with him. He also runs scheduled monthly security and performance rescans for a compliance SaaS.
