◇ FAQ ◇

Frequently interrogated.

Q Is the chat above actually live AI, or scripted?
The terminal above runs in guided mode here — pattern-matched answers for common questions (pricing, timeline, stack, etc.) and URL intake for diagnostics. For the real, live, full-context Claude conversation (memory, file uploads, the works) head to the chat on the homepage. That talks to Claude (Anthropic Sonnet 4.6) running on Cloudflare's edge.
Q How fast do you actually respond?
The AIs answer in under 3 seconds, 24/7. Chris is human, lives in Sydney AEST, and replies to email within 4 business hours — usually faster. Phone calls returned within 90 minutes. Real human response, not a ticket queue.
Q What happens after I drop a URL?
The terminal does a basic reachability check (confirms your site is up), then routes the request to Chris. He runs the real audit personally — Google PageSpeed Insights, SERP analysis, content review, the full 12-page Diagnostic Report. You get it back within 24 hours. Free. No obligation. No follow-up sequences. We won't run fake numbers in your browser — anything you see from us about your site is real or we don't show it.
Q Do you work with non-Australian clients?
Yes — about a third of our work is international (NZ, US, UK). The infrastructure (Cloudflare edge, Anthropic + Google APIs) doesn't care where you are. Chris is in Sydney, but calls happen in your timezone.
Q What if I just need a regular website, not the full AI stack?
Absolutely. About 40% of our clients start with a starter package (Engineered Web Foundations, E-Commerce, Brand Identity) and only later upgrade to a Living Website. We don't push the full stack on every project — only when it makes financial sense for you.
Q What about NDAs / privacy?
We sign NDAs as a default for any project briefing. Conversations on this site are stored encrypted on Cloudflare D1 (AU region), retained for 365 days, never shared. Your data never trains a public AI model. Full export anytime via one SQL command. See The Covenant for the full doctrine.
Q How much does a typical project cost?
It depends entirely on your business, your goals, and how many of our six modules you actually need. The free Diagnostic Scan exists exactly because we won't quote without knowing the situation. Indicatively: most projects range from $4,800 (one starter package) to $48,000+ (full Centaur Covenant). Monthly intelligence subscriptions start at $400.
