05 / Use cases

Three businesses.
Three systems.

Concept scenarios: how we put typical jobs together. The businesses are fictional — the building blocks behind them are exactly what we deliver.

CASE A — CABINET MAKER · 8 STAFFCONCEPT

PROBLEM
Every quote costs an evening: measuring, pricing, writing. Prospects drop off before the quote is finished.
SYSTEM
A website with an enquiry assistant that captures measurements and wishes in a structured way, plus a quote generator built on their own price lists — every quote prepared for sign-off.
GAIN
Days become one business day. The owner reviews and signs instead of typing — and the fastest quote in town wins the job.

CASE B — RETAILER · SHOP + WAREHOUSECONCEPT

PROBLEM
The shop, inventory and shipping don’t know each other. Orders get retyped into Xero, stock counts never match, returns are chaos.
SYSTEM
A store wired into inventory and accounting: payment, invoice, shipping label and stock movement run as one flow — with a log for every step.
GAIN
Orders flow through untouched. Stock is right, the books are right — and the team sells instead of typing.

CASE C — B2B SUPPLIER · REPEAT CUSTOMERSCONCEPT

PROBLEM
Regulars order by phone and email. Sales retypes orders, price lists circulate as stale spreadsheets.
SYSTEM
A customer portal with login: per-customer pricing, order history, two-click reordering — handed straight to order processing and accounting.
GAIN
Customers order themselves, around the clock and error-free. Sales calls for new business — not for typos.
