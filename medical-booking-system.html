◇ The Problem ◇

Four leaks every clinic silently bleeds from.

01
Patients call for basics they've heard 1000 times — "What are your hours?" "Do you bulk bill?" "How do I find you?" Each one costs reception time that should go to patient care.
02
After-hours emergencies can't reach you — They go to your competitor or the ER. By Monday morning, the patient is somewhere else.
03
No-shows waste your most expensive resource — Empty slots = lost revenue. Filling them manually means receptionist phone tag with the waitlist.
04
New patient intake = paperwork mountain — Medicare cards, forms, pre-screening — all done at the desk while three other patients wait.

Sound familiar? Your website should be your first line of care, not your last.
