Questions

Good to know.

Is AI checkout open to Australian stores?

Partly. Google’s Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP) launched in Australia in June 2026 with selected retailers, so shoppers can buy single items inside Search, AI Mode and Gemini (Inside Retail). Google’s Merchant Center Help says checkout covers Australia for select merchants, and you stay the seller of record.

When can we turn UCP on in Merchant Center?

Google’s Merchant Center Help (October 2026) says the self-serve UCP hub is rolling out in the United States first, with Australia and Canada to follow next year. We do the groundwork now: product data, feeds and checkout readiness, so you can apply as soon as it opens.

Can our Shopify store sell in ChatGPT or Gemini?

Shopify’s Agentic Storefronts is on by default for eligible stores and can list products in ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, Gemini, Copilot and Meta. Shopify’s help centre (October 2026) says direct checkout in AI Mode and Gemini, and selling in ChatGPT, currently require selling to United States customers.

Are Australians buying through AI yet?

Deloitte’s 2026 Retail Report found 45% of consumers use AI to compare products and services, and 11% use it to complete or automate purchases. An Ideally survey of 809 Australians (October 2026) found half had used AI to research a purchase.

Are you building a store like this?
