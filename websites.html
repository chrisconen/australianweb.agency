Questions

Good to know.

Do you use AI to build the site?

Yes. AWA is open about it. Architect and Astra, our AI engineers, write, test and review code. Chris Conen supervises that work and is your only point of contact, Australia-wide. There is no account manager between you and the build.

What does accessible mean on a site you build?

We work to the Australian Human Rights Commission’s April 2025 guidelines on equal access to digital goods and services, which recommend WCAG 2.2 Level AA as a minimum. They are not legally binding, and this is not legal advice: for a compliance opinion, ask a lawyer.

How do you judge whether a page is fast?

We use the thresholds in Google's web.dev article on Core Web Vitals, last updated 7 May 2025. At the 75th percentile, Good is LCP at or under 2,500 ms, INP at or under 200 ms, and CLS at or under 0.1. Poor is LCP over 4,000 ms, INP over 500 ms and CLS over 0.25.

Can you build a web app, or only a marketing site?

Both. A landing page when that is the job. A web application when you need accounts, dashboards and admin tools. The same engagement covers interface design, a fast front end and the content structure behind it.
